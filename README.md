# Steam Discount Bot 🎮
Telegram bot for tracking discounts and sales on Steam games. Get notified about price drops, special deals, and major Steam sales events!


Steam Discount Bot helps you track price changes for your favorite games on Steam. The bot monitors game prices and sends notifications when discounts appear or when major Steam sales begin.

✨ Features
Core Functionality
🔍 Game Search: Search for any game in the Steam catalog

📊 Price Tracking: Track price changes and discounts for your favorite games

🔥 Hot Deals: View current featured deals and promotions on Steam

📋 My Games: Manage your tracked games list

💬 Notifications: Get automatic alerts about:

Price drops for tracked games

Major Steam sales events

Special discounts and promotions
